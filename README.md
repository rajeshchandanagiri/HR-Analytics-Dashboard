#  HR Analytics Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project is an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee data and understand workforce trends.

The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, attrition, attrition rate, education, job roles, job satisfaction, age groups, and gender-based attrition.

The objective of this project is to transform HR data into meaningful visual insights that can help organizations understand workforce patterns and monitor employee attrition.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee count and workforce composition.
- Calculate and monitor employee attrition.
- Analyze overall attrition rate.
- Understand employee distribution across age groups.
- Analyze attrition across different departments.
- Compare attrition across education fields.
- Analyze job satisfaction ratings by job role.
- Compare attrition patterns between male and female employees.
- Create an interactive HR analytics dashboard.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Department-wise Attrition

Analyzes employee attrition across major departments:

- Research & Development
- Sales
- Human Resources

This helps identify how attrition is distributed across departments.

### 2. Employee Distribution by Age Group

Shows the number of employees across different age groups:

- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Above 55

The visualization also provides a gender-based breakdown.

### 3. Job Satisfaction Analysis

Displays job satisfaction ratings from 1 to 4 across different job roles.

Job roles include:

- Healthcare Representative
- Human Resources
- Laboratory Technician
- Manager
- Manufacturing Director
- Research Director
- Research Scientist
- Sales Executive
- Sales Representative

### 4. Education Field-wise Attrition

Analyzes attrition across different education fields, including:

- Life Sciences
- Medical
- Marketing
- Technical Degree
- Other
- Human Resources

### 5. Attrition Rate by Gender and Age Group

Analyzes attrition across different age groups and compares male and female employees.

This provides a more detailed view of workforce attrition patterns.

### 6. Interactive Education Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for education levels such as:

- Associate's Degree
- Bachelor's Degree
- Master's Degree
- Doctoral Degree
- High School

Users can select different education levels to explore the dashboard dynamically.

## Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify patterns such as:

- Overall employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Differences in attrition across departments.
- Employee distribution across different age groups.
- Education fields with higher numbers of employees leaving.
- Job roles with different levels of job satisfaction.
- Gender-based differences in attrition across age groups.

These insights can help HR teams better understand workforce trends and identify areas that may require further investigation.
